ABOUT
CUT THE FLOW is a browser-based video editor. No upload, no server — everything runs locally in your browser. Import media files, arrange them on the timeline, add effects and transitions, then export your final video.

TIPS
  • Drag & drop files directly into the media library
  • Right-click a clip for context menu options
  • Use the scroll wheel on the timeline to zoom
  • Projects are auto-saved to your browser storage
  • Export supports multiple engines (Canvas, WebCodecs, Transmux)
